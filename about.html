Our History

Founded on Security.
Built on Trust.

Aurum was established in Geneva in 1987 by a consortium of former Swiss National Bank security officers and international logistics experts. From our first bullion transfer between Switzerland and South Africa, we built a reputation that sovereign institutions have relied upon ever since.

Today, Aurum operates a network of 14 secure processing hubs across six continents, employing over 180 certified security logistics specialists. We hold the highest industry certifications and maintain a zero-breach record across 37 years of operation.

Our clientele includes central banks, international mining conglomerates, luxury maisons, fine art institutions, and UHNW private individuals who demand the absolute highest standard of discretion and care.

Security Framework

Multi-Layer Security Architecture

Every Aurum shipment is protected by our proprietary 7-Layer Security Protocol — developed over three decades and continuously updated.

01
Physical Security

Armored vehicles, armed escorts, and tamper-evident packaging on every shipment regardless of route or size.

02
Digital Surveillance

Real-time GPS + RFID tracking with encrypted data streams monitored 24/7 from our Geneva Control Center.

03
Identity Verification

Biometric identity verification for all handlers, with chain-of-custody documentation at every transfer point.

04
Route Intelligence

Dynamic route optimization using live threat intelligence from 80+ countries, updated every 15 minutes.

05
Vault Infrastructure

Class III certified vault facilities with seismic sensors, dual-biometric access, and insurance up to $500M per consignment.

06
Compliance & Documentation

Full regulatory compliance in every jurisdiction, with expert customs brokers and CITES/Kimberley documentation specialists.
